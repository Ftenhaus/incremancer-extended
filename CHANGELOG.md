# Changelog
## 0.5.0 — Skeleton Champion progression and equipment
- Equipment tooltips show equipped slot comparisons and salvage XP including the champion XP rate. Bulk salvage protects equipped and high-rarity gear. Equipment grants XP while the champion is down.
- Exact XP thresholds grant levels. Large awards can grant up to 100 levels per update, with remaining XP carried forward.
## 0.4.0 — shop automation and progression statistics
- Pause and resume automatic shop and prestige purchases from Options after unlocking automation. Stats shows the highest cleared level and counts automatic ranks purchased since this update.

## 0.3.0 — economy development build
- Discounted generator and upgrade ranks with a positive base price cost at least one unit.
- Added a reproducible prestige and ascension production simulation.

## 0.2.0 — numeric limits
- Corrected rounded bulk prices, high-rank prestige production, brain prestige selection and late enemy health transitions.

## 0.1.0 — technical foundation (development baseline)
- Central balance settings, isolated local saves, upstream cloud login disabled.
- Regression tests and CI, Extended branding.
- Browser smoke tests and save round-trip validation outstanding; no public deployment approved.
